Help Topics

Welcome to Denarii

Denarii is a trading reflection tool. Your journal is stored only on this computer, encrypted with your password.

Use it to plan your trading day, log trades, reflect on execution, track behavioral patterns, and spot what's working or costing you money. The core workflow is Plan → Trade → Reflect → Improve.

This page covers what's available in the current beta.

Getting Started

Create Your Account

Choose a username and a password. You'll receive a recovery key. Save it now. It is the only way to reset your password.

Moving to Another Computer

Go to Settings → Data → Back up all data, then use Restore from backup in Settings → Data on the new computer.

Navigate

The sidebar groups pages under Reflections, Planning, Strategy & Tags, Analytics, and AI Analysis. Click a group to expand it. Press Ctrl+K (or Cmd+K) to jump anywhere fast.

Lock Your Session

Click Lock in the top bar or Logout from the profile menu. Your journal stays on this computer; sign back in to pick up where you left off.

Back Up Your Data

Go to Settings → Data and click Back up all data (.sqlite). Export journal (JSON) in the profile menu is an extra copy of one account. See Backup & Export for details.

Using the Demo Account

A demo broker named Pelosi with sample trades is included in your account so you can explore Denarii without entering real data. Find it under Settings → Broker Accounts.

It is visible by default. Click the eye button on its card to hide it from the app or show it again. Click Restore Demo in Settings → Broker Accounts to refresh it with fresh sample data.

Adding & Editing Trades

Log a New Trade

Open New Trade from the sidebar. Enter symbol, side, quantity, prices, and timestamps. Options fields appear when you select an options asset type.

  • Planned Levels: your initial target and other planned levels for the trade.
  • Trade Grade: rate how well you executed.
  • Strategy Rules: check which playbook rules you followed.
  • Tags: custom labels to categorize the trade.
  • Notes: rich text for detailed post-trade reflection.
  • Quick Notes: a short note saved with the trade.
  • Calculated Metrics: trade risk, planned and realized R, drawdown and runup, filled in from your entries.

Drafts auto-save as you type, so you won't lose work if you navigate away.

Import from a Broker

Click Broker Import to load trades from a CSV. CSV import supports Webull, Robinhood and thinkorswim; tastytrade and IBKR are coming soon.

Edit an Existing Trade

Click any trade in Trade Reflection to open it. Strategy, planning, and notes are always editable. Execution fields (price, quantity, timestamps) are locked by default to preserve the original record. Click Unlock Fields to correct them.

On Trade Reflection you can also edit individual fills, and split a trade by marking fill rows to move into a new trade. Clean Up with AI and dictation are available in the notes (see Dictation & AI Clean Up).

  • AI Analysis: on Trade Reflection, get AI feedback on the trade. Click Append to Reflection Notes to add it to your notes.

Reflections

Trade Reflection

Lists all your trades. Filter by date range, strategy, symbol, or style. Click any trade to open its full reflection entry where you can review execution, update notes, and grade your performance.

Periodic Reflections

Four reflection cadences with guided prompts, KPI summaries, and context widgets:

  • Daily Reflection: what worked, what didn't, tomorrow's plan.
  • Weekly Reflection: patterns, best/worst behavior, next week's focus.
  • Monthly Reflection: edge strength, habits to build or break.
  • Yearly Reflection: growth retrospective, lessons, goals ahead.

All reflections auto-save as you type. Find them under the Reflections group in the sidebar.

Planning

Planning pages help you prepare before the market opens so you trade with intention instead of reacting in the moment. They are part of the Plan → Trade → Reflect → Improve workflow.

Daily Planner

Record your market bias and expected condition, key events and catalysts, and your watchlist, then write your plan for the day and set your Behavioral Focus (Continue, Stop, Focus). Completing this before the open keeps you focused on your highest-conviction strategies.

Weekly Planner

Record the current market condition, what you expect next week, key events and news catalysts, and key market guides, then write your plan for the week and set your Behavioral Focus. Use it on weekends or before Monday's session to establish context for your daily plans.

Weekly Planner and Weekly Reflection weeks run Sunday to Saturday. Each planner has a Run AI button that reviews your plan. Click Append to Plan to add the AI review to your plan.

Find both planners under the Planning group in the sidebar. Plans auto-save as you work.

Dictation & AI Clean Up

Dictate Instead of Typing

Every notes editor has a microphone button in its toolbar: New Trade, Trade Reflection, the Daily, Weekly, Monthly and Yearly Reflections, and the Daily and Weekly Planners. To dictate, click in the editor and press Windows key + H. The first time, click the microphone in the Windows voice typing box. Clicking the microphone button shows this tip.

Clean Up with AI

Dictated or quickly typed notes can be messy. Click Clean Up with AI next to the editor to turn them into a tidy, organized version.

  • Choose Bullets or Paragraphs from the dropdown first. Your choice is remembered.
  • The cleaned-up version is added below your notes, labeled "Cleaned up by AI" with the time. Your original text is never changed, so delete whichever part you don't want.
  • Clean Up fixes spelling, punctuation and filler words, uppercases tickers, and groups your notes under headings. It won't add trade ideas, levels or opinions you didn't write. Anything unclear is marked [unclear: ...].
  • Running it again starts from your original text, not from an earlier cleanup.

Clean Up is available on New Trade, Trade Reflection, all four period Reflections, and both Planners. The Planners keep their own Bullets/Paragraphs setting. It needs your own Gemini API key (see Gemini API Key).

Filters & Sorting

  • Account filter (top bar): scope the entire app to one broker account or view all.
  • Period filter: All, YTD, Month, Week, Today, or Custom (available on most pages). Weeks are calendar weeks starting Sunday.
  • Style / Asset Type / Strategy / Symbol: narrow results for deeper drill-down. Setups are filtered by Strategy.
  • Column headers: click to sort in tables and trade lists.

Themes

Strategies, Tags & Templates

Strategy Management

Build your playbook under Strategy Management. Each strategy has rule groups (e.g., Entry Criteria, Risk Management) whose rules appear as checkboxes during trade entry so you can score execution.

Favorite the strategies you actively trade. Unfavoriting hides a strategy from dropdowns but doesn't delete it. Use + Add Favorite to bring it back.

Tags

Create custom labels under Settings → Tags Management and apply them to trades during entry or editing. Organize by category (pattern, mistake, catalyst, etc.).

Note Templates

Insert a template from the notes toolbar to start with a consistent structure instead of a blank editor. Favorite a template to make it appear in the quick-pick dropdown.

Analytics & AI Analysis

Analytics

All analytics pages are under the Analytics group in the sidebar:

  • Snapshot: customizable widget dashboard. Add, resize, and rearrange.
  • Performance Trends: equity curve, P&L bars, win rate, and risk metrics over time.
  • Strategy: which strategies have edge and which are leaking money.
  • Timing: day-of-week and time-of-day performance.
  • Symbols: tickers classified as EDGE, NEUTRAL, LEAK, or HEMORRHAGE.
  • Risk: sizing analysis, blowup days, stop-loss modeling, damage concentration.
  • Discipline: revenge trading, FOMO, premature exits, tilt (with estimated dollar cost).

Performance Trends, Strategy and Timing have a ? button that explains their metrics.

AI Analysis

Gemini-powered interpretation of your trade data. Found under the AI Analysis group in the sidebar:

  • Prompt Library: browse built-in analysis prompts or create your own.
  • Run Analysis: pick a prompt, set filters, and run it against your trades.
  • Saved Reports: revisit previously generated AI reports. When you run an analysis, you can save the output here for later review. Useful for tracking how insights change over time.

AI features need your own Gemini API key. There is no built-in key. See Gemini API Key to add one.

Gemini API Key

AI features need your own Gemini API key. There is no built-in key, so AI features stay off until you add one.

Adding Your Own Key

  • Open the profile menu (bottom-left of the sidebar).
  • Click Settings.
  • Go to the Integrations tab.
  • Paste your Gemini API key into the Gemini API Key field.
  • Click Save Key.

After saving, the key will not be shown again for security. To replace it, paste a new key and click Save Key again.

Removing Your Key

Click Remove Key on the same Integrations page. Removing the key turns AI features off.

Security

  • Keep your API key private. Do not share it or post it publicly.
  • Your key is stored encrypted on this computer. It is only sent to Google's Gemini API when you use an AI feature.

Get a Gemini API Key

AI features need your own Gemini API key. There is no built-in key. Your key is stored encrypted on this computer.

Part 1: Create Your Gemini API Key

Step 1: Open Google AI Studio
Sign in to Google AI Studio using your Google account.

Step 2: Go to API Keys
Open the API Keys section in Google AI Studio.

Step 3: Create or choose a project
If prompted, select an existing Google Cloud project or create a new one.

Step 4: Create a new API key
Create a Gemini API key for that project.

Step 5: Copy the key
Copy the full API key and keep it private.

Important: Keep Your Key Safe

  • Do not share your Gemini API key.
  • Do not post it publicly.
  • Do not include it in screenshots.
  • Do not save it in public GitHub repositories.

If you think your key was exposed, generate a new one in Google AI Studio and replace it in Denarii.

Part 2: Add the Key in Denarii

  • Open the profile menu (bottom-left of the sidebar).
  • Click Settings.
  • Open the Integrations tab.
  • Find Gemini API Key.
  • Paste your key.
  • Click Save Key.

After saving, the key will not be shown again for security. To replace it, paste a new key and click Save Key again.

Removing the key turns AI features off.

Connect Webull

Denarii can import your Webull trades automatically using your own Webull OpenAPI key. It only reads your accounts and order history and never places orders.

Before You Start

  • Apply for Webull OpenAPI access in Webull's developer portal.
  • Generate your own App Key and App Secret.
  • Turn on the Trading permission. Webull requires it to read order history, but Denarii does not trade.

One key covers all of your Denarii broker accounts.

Connect

  • Open the profile menu, click Settings, then open the Integrations tab.
  • Under Webull, paste your App Key and App Secret.
  • Choose Import trades starting from, or leave it empty to import all history.
  • Click Connect, then approve Denarii in the Webull app.
  • Back in Denarii, click "I've approved it, check again".

When it's connected you'll see "Webull connected" with the last 4 characters of your key, your Webull accounts, and the date your approval expires.

Your key and secret are stored encrypted on this computer only and are never shown again after you connect.

When Approval Expires

An approval lasts about 15 days. The expiry date turns amber when it's within 3 days. When it expires, syncing pauses and the account shows "Needs approval". To renew, click Send new code, approve in the Webull app, then click "I've approved it, check again".

Disconnect

Click Disconnect on the Integrations tab. Trades you already imported stay as they are. Also revoke the App Key in Webull's developer portal, because removing it from Denarii does not invalidate it at Webull.

Webull Sync & Auto-Import

After Webull is connected, link each Denarii broker account to one Webull account.

Link an Account

  • Go to Settings, then Broker Accounts, and click Edit on a Webull account. (Save a new account first, then click Edit.)
  • In the Webull link section, choose the Linked Webull account.
  • Check Import trades starting from. It starts as the date you chose when connecting. Leave it empty to import all history.
  • Click Save Account. The first import starts right away and shows its progress.

When It Syncs

  • When you open Denarii and sign in.
  • Every 15 minutes while Denarii is open.
  • When you click Sync now in the Webull link section.

The section shows when it last synced, the status, and a summary of the last sync (imported, updated, held for review, skipped).

Your Edits Are Protected

  • Sync never deletes trades, and a trade you delete is never imported again.
  • Closed trades are never changed after import.
  • On open trades, your notes, tags, grades and strategy are always kept.
  • If you change an open trade's quantity, price, time or fees, sync stops updating it and lists it under Needs review.

Needs Review

When sync holds something back, the account card shows "Webull: N need review". Click Edit to see each item in the Webull link section:

  • Edited after import: choose Keep my edits to make your values the new starting point and apply any Webull fills that were waiting, or Use Webull's data to put Webull's values back. Notes, tags and strategy are kept either way.
  • Before earliest Webull trade: fills found after you moved the start date earlier. Import adds them when they form complete trades of their own; otherwise Denarii explains why and keeps them held. Ignore skips them for good.

Good to Know

  • Moving the start date earlier imports older history. Moving it later only affects future syncs.
  • Denarii only reads from Webull. It never places, changes or cancels orders.
  • If you see "Needs approval", renew the approval (see Connect Webull).

Backup & Export

Your data is stored only on this computer. Keep a backup somewhere safe in case this computer fails.

How to Back Up

  • Open Settings, then the Data tab.
  • Click Back up all data (.sqlite). This saves every account on this computer.
  • Choose where to save the file. Somewhere off this computer, such as a USB drive, is safest.
  • For an extra copy of one account, use Export journal (JSON) in the profile menu.

When to Back Up

  • After adding or editing a batch of trades.
  • Before reinstalling or moving to a new computer.
  • Before any major Denarii update.
  • Occasionally, for your own records.

How to Restore

To restore everything, use Restore from backup in Settings → Data. To restore a JSON export, use Recover from Backup on the login screen.

Known Limitations (Beta)

  • Broker import: CSV import works for Webull, Robinhood and thinkorswim; tastytrade and IBKR are not active yet.
  • Stock charts: depend on the Twelve Data API. Availability varies by plan.
  • AI features: require internet and Gemini API availability. Temporary rate limits can occur.
